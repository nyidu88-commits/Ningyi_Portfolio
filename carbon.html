Climate Finance Gap Explorer
Environmental Defense Fund · Climate Finance Intern · Dec 2025 – Present
  • Built on the climate finance gap framework proposed by Kerr & Hu (2025, npj Climate Action), using three factors (carbon price level, coverage, leverage ratio) across BAU, ICM and ICM+HLR scenarios; independently delivered an interactive climate finance data visualization tool (R Shiny) end-to-end, from requirements analysis through design, development and launch, supporting multi-scenario forecasting as a data-driven decision-support tool for policy researchers.
  • Open-sourced the complete codebase on GitHub so other researchers can reuse the analytical framework, reflecting an emphasis on extensible design and engineering rigor.

Southeast Asia Finance Project: Article 6 Carbon Credit Trading and Regional Financing Gaps
Environmental Defense Fund · 2026 – Present
  • Mapped how much climate finance Southeast Asia needs versus what it actually receives: the region needs about $420 billion a year to cut emissions, but only $39.5 billion came in during 2024, leaving a gap of roughly $380 billion a year.
  • Tested how much carbon markets can help. Today's carbon taxes and trading schemes, Article 6 deals included, could mobilise only about $2.5 billion a year, less than 1% of the gap.
  • Looked at why clean power investment is lagging: three-quarters of the region's electricity still comes from fossil fuels, power prices are held down by subsidies, and few countries have set a date to phase out coal.
